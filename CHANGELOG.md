# Changelog

All notable changes to Zombie Hunter. Versions follow [SemVer](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Changed
- **Safehouse rebuilt as Relay Station 9** (design-safehouse.md): the basement of the old radio station that ran the towers, 64 x 48 studs under a low 16-stud ceiling. The center is warm (an oil-drum fire with embers, four army cots, crates, a sandbag half-ring, candles, the one hanging bulb with moths around it, a patch of old studded tiles); the edges are cold. You spawn at the foot of a stairwell climbing into pure black, facing the fire. Every station is an object: the map vote is a chalkboard with chalk tally marks and three chalk-outlined pads; the Alpha pick is three murky specimen jars (RUNNER / TANK / SCREAMER, "Choose your nightmare", nothing changes in the world so the pick stays secret); the shop is a chain-link cage with a brass bell to ring under a sick green tube; the catalog is a cracked mirror behind a half-drawn curtain. Also: a loadout pegboard and lockers, a radio desk with a reel-to-reel, a CRT of static and four CCTV monitors, the tape shelf, two boarded windows with lavender moonbeams cut into stripes by the boards and dust in the light, the steel deploy door with its wheel lock and red caged bulb, and a corkboard of **MISSING** posters that shows the faces and names of the players infected last round.
  - **Scare director (client only, `SafehouseController` + `Rules/LobbyScares`, 8 tests):** at most one scare every 35-70s, never while the shop or dressing room is open, never in your first 20s, never during the final countdown: bangs on the boards and a hand between the planks, the bulb dimming, dust sifting down as footsteps cross the ceiling, the door wheel turning by itself, eyes in the boards, and rarely the Tall One standing in the moonbeam. Each comes with a caption. The fire light wavers, the jars breathe, the CRT shimmers, the shop tube stutters.
  - **Countdown:** at 10s the door bulb pulses; at 5s the hanging bulb settles dim, candles gutter and breath fogs at the door; at 3s the wheel lock spins; at the end the door swings open into lavender fog, and closes behind you once the round is under way.
  - Not in yet (need assets or systems that don't exist): sounds and music, MaterialVariants / SurfaceAppearance textures, the loadout / archive / stats / spectate panels (their objects are props for now), camera moments, first-time footprints, the mirror-lag / poster-swap / radio-whisper scares.
- **New safehouse (the waiting room):** the toy-like studded purple room is now a realistic survivors' safehouse at night, built like the maps (`Maps/Safehouse`, out of `MapService`). A crackling fireplace with candles and a clock stopped at 3:33 (the WAITING FOR PLAYERS / NEXT ROUND sign hangs on its chimney), a leather couch, armchairs and a coffee table on a red rug, warm hanging lamps (one flickers), bookshelves, an old TV showing static, dust drifting in the light. The vote board is a corkboard with paper posters under little picture lights and soft glowing pads in front; the Z-Shop is a supply counter with stocked shelves; the catalog is a framed dressing mirror with a clothes rack; the Alpha pedestals stand in a dark alcove under red spotlights. A little scary: boarded windows with a dark figure pressing its hands on the glass, a barricaded back door with claw marks, bloody handprints and DON'T LET THEM IN scrawled on the wall. Everything works as before (same prompts, same spawn), and the headless build test now checks every station is there.
- **Screen code split by job (no behaviour change):** the ~970-line card module is now a ~100-line queue (`AnnouncementController`) plus `Cards/Screen` (the card screen and its effects), `Cards/IntroCards`, `Cards/EndCards` and `Cards/Banners`; cards and banners are registered in tables, so adding one no longer touches the queue. The ~930-line HUD is now a ~130-line coordinator (`HudController`) plus one module per part of the screen in `Hud/`: TopBar, PlayerList, Vitals, Weapons, Objectives, RoleHelp, LobbyInfo, Effects and Prompts. Each builds its own widgets and redraws from one snapshot of the round per frame. The unused `HudController.myRole` is gone.
- **Code clean-up (design review):** one tested rule for "free to use the lobby / sitting the round out" (`Rules/Lobby`, 4 tests) replaces five copies in the shop, dressing room, HUD and server; `Outfit.replace` (tested) replaces three hand-written outfit copies in the catalog; one client `CharacterCopy` module copies and zombifies characters for the role cards, the recap and the room previews (it was written three times); the old classless Alpha stats and the unused `AlphaStart` map spot are gone, so custom maps no longer need an `AlphaStart` part.
- **Adventurer starters:** the boy now wears the free Roblox *Denim Jacket with White Hoodie*, *Dark Green Jeans* and *Blonde Spiked Hair*; the girl the free *Blue Plaid Shirt*, *Black Jeans with White Shoes* and *Brown Charmer Hair* (all made by Roblox, price 0, checked in the catalog). Every character also gets adventurer gear built from parts: a worn backpack with a bedroll, shoulder straps and a little flashlight clipped to the chest (disguised zombies too, so it gives nothing away).
- **Slower role reveal, longer break:** the role card stays 8s and its one-line explanation types at half speed; the rules card stays 7s and types slower; the Hunter / Everyone armed card 2.5s. The intro is now 29s (`introLength`, head start 39s). The countdown in the safehouse before the next round is now **60s** (`intermission`), so players rest about 80s between rounds.
- **Friendly fire:** if the Hunter shoots a human (survivor, or a second Hunter), that player is killed and the Hunter dies too. The gun drops and glows where the Hunter fell (banner **THE HUNTER SHOT AN INNOCENT - BOTH ARE DEAD. GRAB THE GUN!**), and the first survivor to pick it up becomes the new Hunter, with the same rule. Private cards: **YOU SHOT AN INNOCENT** / **THE HUNTER SHOT YOU**. The dead watch from the safehouse (they can shop), don't count as humans alive, get no win bonus, and show as **KILLED** in the recap. Shooting zombies works as before. 5 tests.
- **Round recap:** after the result, a slow card lines up every player's character in the dark; one by one a light comes on over each with their name, the role they really had (the Alpha and Hunter are revealed now that the round is over), what happened to them (**SURVIVED** / **ESCAPED** / **INFECTED** / **THE ALPHA**) and **WINNER** for the winning side. Infected players and the Alpha are shown as zombies. Winners come first and stay lit; the rest fade. The whole end sequence is slower (result 4s, recap about 8-12s, coins 3s; results phase 20s). `RoundRules.recap`, 4 tests.
- **Cleaner HUD design:** one glass style everywhere (dark see-through plum, soft rounded corners, a thin lavender edge, softer text outlines). The clock sits in a pill at the top centre in a clean font with one quiet line under it; coins and the role help icon top left; PLAYERS and SHOP pills top right with the list under them; health + medkit bottom left; repair bar bottom centre (slim, like health); ammo, ability and reload bottom right; smaller point popups. Chips only appear when they have something to say.
- **Role help icon:** during a round a round **?** icon in your role colour (top left, with your role name) opens a panel with your goal and how to play, keys included (**H** on a keyboard). It follows your role (infected → zombie tips) and the mode (Outbreak survivors). Words live in `Config/RoleGuide`.
- **Slower, creepier role pick:** the intro is now 22s (`introLength`, head start 32s). SOMEONE IS INFECTED types in slowly with whispers while the red light drifts between the shadows and sometimes lingers; YOUR ROLE has a 5-beat heartbeat in the dark before the light stutters on, then a quick one-line explanation of your role under the title.
- **Straight to the safehouse:** the moment a round ends everyone is brought back to the safehouse, and the result and coin cards play there. Players rest at least 35s before the next round (15s results + 20s countdown); a test checks the countdown never drops under 20s.
- **Last-minute clock:** under 1:00 the timer turns a deep blood red with a dark edge, and no longer shakes.
- **Spread-out group starts:** at round start players are split into groups of 2 (the last group may have 3), and each group starts at its own spot, far apart on the map (`Rules/SpawnGroups`, 6 tests). Groups are reshuffled every round and the disguised Alpha lands in a random group. The greybox Town now has 7 `HumanStart` spots; a map with one spot still starts everyone together.
- **Role reveal emotes:** on the intro's role card your character acts out what it got, as the light comes on: **Survivor** crouches, shields its face and shakes, head snapping around; **Hunter** raises a revolver, scans and fires (recoil + muzzle flash); **Alpha** hangs limp, then its head snaps up crooked, red eyes open, claws rise and it glitches; **Zombie** (the infected card) shambles with arms reaching, head lolling and mint eyes. It carries on into the rules card. Made in code (`RoleEmote`), R6 and R15.
- **Repair emote:** while repairing a radio tower your character turns to face the box, holds a wrench and hammers at it (left hand steadying), with sparks on every hit. Everyone sees it. Made in code (no animation asset), works on R6 and R15.
- **Slimmer health bar:** a small, thin rounded bar in the bottom-left corner with a caption above it (HEALTHY / WOUNDED x1 / HP for zombies). It slides smoothly on a hit, humans' bar is split into 3 hit marks, and it turns pink when you're one hit from going down. The medkit line sits just above it.
- **Every zombie is disguised:** infected (basic) zombies now look like any other player too (own skin and clothes, a flashlight). Claws start in the backpack: take them out (**1**, or tap them in the toolbar) to attack, and your zombie face (zombie skin + glowing eyes) shows **for as long as the claws are out**; put them away and you look human again. The Alpha's revealed eyes are red, and its claws can't be taken out during the head start. Alpha abilities still show the face for 3s. Basic zombies keep their slower speed.
- **The next mode is a surprise:** the lobby no longer says which mode is next (HUD shows NEXT ROUND STARTING, the sign shows NEXT ROUND STARTING SOON / ROUND IN PROGRESS). You find out on the intro's MODE card.

### Fixed
- **Play test stuck in the sky (no safehouse, no character):** the server crashed while building the safehouse because the mode sign's colour was lost when the maps moved into their own modules. The colour is back, and a new headless test (`MapBuild.spec`, 5 tests) builds the safehouse and every map at server start with real Roblox instances, so this kind of crash now fails the tests instead of showing up in Studio.
- **Client controllers start independently:** each one (HUD, cards, weapons, timing, repair emote, shop, catalog) now starts on its own, so if one fails the others still run, and the Output shows `[Zombie Hunter] <name> failed to start: ...`. A failing controller started earlier could have kept the repair emote from ever starting.
- **Your items gave your role away before the reveal:** everyone was spawned with their role's tools (gun, medkit, claws) at the start of the intro, and Roblox's toolbar shows them above the intro cards. Now everyone spawns empty-handed, and the tools (and the Hunter's ammo) arrive when the intro ends.
- **Everyone still started together with 2-3 players:** the group starts made only one group below 4 players. Now there are always at least 2 starting spots when 2+ players play (2 players start apart, 3 players as 1 + 2).
- **Empty bar in the waiting room:** the tower repair bar showed whenever you weren't repairing (a `false ~= nil` check). It now only shows while you repair a tower.
- **Card text pacing:** a card never switches before its text has finished typing plus 1.2s to read it. The role pick's whispers are fewer and slower (two lines, each left up to read), and its last line waits for them. Intro is now 32s (head start 42s).
- No green bar when you join: the health bar stays hidden until a round, and Roblox's own health bar is turned off.
- Repair emote: your own repair now also starts it straight from your round state (it no longer depends only on the server's signal), and the Output warns if the character has no arm joints to animate.
- **Gun aiming:** the crosshair now follows your mouse cursor (centre of the screen on phones and in shift-lock), the mouse pointer hides while you hold a gun, and the shot goes exactly to the point under the crosshair. Bullets no longer stop on invisible parts.

- **No roles in the player list:** other players are always blank in the list, even infected zombies, downed or escaped players (only WAITING for people sitting out the round). Your own row still shows your role. The server no longer sends anyone else's role, wounds or downed state to your client.
- **Disguised Alpha:** the Alpha looks, moves and lights up like any survivor (own skin, flashlight, human speed) and starts with them. Every claw swing shows his zombie face (skin, red eyes, Alpha speed) for 3s (`Weapons.AlphaReveal`), then he blends in again. Claws only work after release.
- Survivors carry no weapons (the bat is Hunter-only).
- The Alpha is the same height as all other players (no longer 1.3x bigger).

### Added
- **Maps upgraded: weather, storms and scares** (ideas from the design document).
  - **Weather:** drizzle (Town, Forest Camp, Mall) or heavy rain (School) falls around every player and stops when there is a roof over your head; a low mist hugs the ground at ankle height (thickest in the forest). Set per map in the lighting preset (`weather`, `groundFog`), drawn on each client (`AtmosphereController`).
  - **Thunderstorms** on Town, School and Mall: every 2-3 minutes the sky flashes twice, the camera shakes, and every light on the map goes out for 1.5 seconds before coming back (`AtmosphereService`).
  - **The shadow figure:** every 40-90 seconds a tall, thin silhouette with faint eyes may stand on a roof edge, at the end of a hallway, across the lake or at a gallery railing, off to the side of where you're looking. Look straight at it (or walk up to it) and it's gone. Only you see yours. Each map tags 9-12 `ScareSpot`s; the rules are pure and tested (`Rules/Scares`, 7 tests).

In the shop, click an item card to see it on your character (the weapon in your hand, or your zombie skin). **While either room is open you sit the next round out** (the server leaves you out, the countdown only counts ready players, and its intro cards don't play over you); close it with DONE and you join the round after. 4 tests.

The dressing room's **STARTER** tab switches between them, and **RESET TO STARTER** goes back. Colours only for now: add hair / clothes asset ids to `Outfit.STARTERS` to give them real hair and clothes.
- **Character catalog (VH2-style):** everyone plays as their own in-game character, saved in their profile. **New players get the default character** (classic 2014: yellow head and arms, blue torso, green legs) until they change it. Open the catalog at the **dressing mirror** in the safehouse (E) or the **CATALOG** button in the lobby:
  - Tabs HATS, HAIR, FACE, EXTRAS (neck/shoulder/front/back/waist), SHIRTS, PANTS, T-SHIRTS, FACES: search the real Roblox catalog, **WEAR** anything for free, **REMOVE** it, or **BUY** it for real (Roblox's own purchase prompt).
  - **BODY:** 24 colours for all / head / torso / arms / legs. **WEARING:** everything you have on.
  - **RESET TO DEFAULT** and **USE MY ROBLOX AVATAR** (copies your avatar's classic items and colours).
  - The server checks every item with Roblox: only classic R6 items (no layered clothing, no body parts), max 8 accessories (3 per slot). Locked while you're playing a round; works in the safehouse between rounds or while sitting out. Infected players keep their outfit with the zombie skin on top. `Shared/Data/Outfit`, `CatalogService`, `CatalogController`, 8 tests.
- **Z-Shop** (v0.4): press **E** at the shop counter in the safehouse (or the **SHOP** button in the lobby). Tabs **WEAPONS** and **ZOMBIE SKINS**; each card shows the price and **BUY** / **EQUIP** / **EQUIPPED**. Not enough coins shows how many more you need. Shopping works between rounds or while you sit out a round. Items (`Config/Items`), all side-grades or looks:
  - Hunter guns: Revolver (starter), **Lever Rifle** 400 (50 dmg, far, slow, 5 shots), **Rusty SMG** 600 (fast, weak, short range).
  - Hunter melee: Bat (starter), **Shovel** 250 (harder, slower), **Crowbar** 250 (quick, shorter).
  - Zombie skins: Rotten (starter), **Pale Ghoul** 150, **Bog Mint** 150, **Bruised Plum** 200, **Candy Corpse** 300: body color + eye glow for your basic zombie and the Alpha's revealed face (the Alpha's eyes stay red).

- **Saved progress** (v0.4): every player has a saved profile (`Shared/Data/Profile`): **Z-Coins**, owned and equipped items, your Alpha class, and stats (rounds, wins, escapes, survivals, zombie kills, infections, revives, towers repaired, rounds as Hunter / Alpha). Round-end coins are added and saved. `DataService` uses a session lock so two servers never write the same player (`Shared/Data/ProfileStore`), autosaves every 2 min, saves on leave and on shutdown. Your coins show at the top left. If DataStores aren't available (Studio without API access) you still play, with a warning that nothing is saved. 10 new tests (129 total).
- **Safehouse lobby** (v0.3): a bigger, cozy room in the sky: wood floor, rug, couch and an old TV, warm light and pastel string lights, two windows with a zombie peeking in, a shop counter and a dressing mirror (both "soon", v0.4), the Alpha pedestals, and a **NEXT ROUND / NOW PLAYING** sign.

Most votes wins, ties are random, and a map can't be played more than twice in a row. The winning map's name is on the intro card. Only the Town greybox exists so far, so the board shows one map until Mall / School / Forest Camp are built (v1.0). 7 new tests (119 total).
- **Game modes** (v0.3): the mode is picked at random every round (`Config/Modes`, `Rules/ModePicker`): Classic 40 / Outbreak 20, a mode needs its minimum players (Outbreak 6+), and a special mode never comes twice in a row. - **Outbreak mode:** 2 Alphas (3 at 12+), no Hunter, every Survivor carries a **pistol** (20 dmg, 8 / 24, ammo boxes work for them), no towers or helicopter, survive **4:00**, downed players turn after **10s**. The intro ends with **EVERYONE IS ARMED** instead of the Hunter card. Survivors earn zombie-kill points in Outbreak. 12 new tests.
- **Alpha classes** (v0.3): pick **Runner**, **Tank** or **Screamer** at the pedestals in the lobby; it's used whenever you're the Alpha (default Runner, private). Runner 300 HP + **DASH** (speed burst), Tank 600 HP + **SLAM** (knocks nearby humans back and slows them), Screamer 350 HP + **SCREAM** (nearby humans glow through walls for every zombie, 5s). Press **Q** or tap the ability button; each ability has a cooldown and shows the Alpha's face like an attack. Numbers in `Config/AlphaClasses`. 7 new tests.
- **Phase 2 objectives:**
  - Radio towers: each round picks 5 of the map's 8 tower spots (spread out). Hold E to repair (60s alone, +60% per extra repairer). Random skill checks while repairing (same timing bar): good = +2%, failed = -8% and a noise ping for zombies. Walk away, get hit, get downed or hide and the repair stops. Blinking red beacons are visible through the fog and turn mint when repaired.
  - Rescue helicopter: 3 repaired towers call it (one of 3 zones); it lands after 45s and waits 20s. Humans who reach the glowing zone escape (+40) and wait safe in the lobby. If it leaves with anyone on board, or every remaining human has escaped or turned: SURVIVORS ESCAPED.
  - Lockers (14 on the greybox): hide (claws can't reach you, repairs stop), leave with E / LEAVE; zombies can search a locker and pull you out (+5).
  - Medkit: each Survivor gets one; click to heal one hit off the nearest hurt teammate (+5) or yourself.
  - Points: 1 per 10% repaired, +25 tower, +40 escape, +5 zombie for stopping a repair / finding a hider, +5 heal.
  - HUD: towers x/3, helicopter countdown, repair bar, hiding screen, medkit count, zombie noise pings. Banners for towers and helicopter, YOU ESCAPED card.
  - 25 new Lune tests (93 total): SpotPicker, Objectives, Survival.
- Creepier round intro: VHS scanlines, flickering lights, glitching titles, typed text; a MODE card; every player shown as a faceless shadow while "someone is infected" (a red light jumps between them and stops on nobody); your own character revealed in the dark with a heartbeat for your role card (red flash + shake for the Alpha); the infected card shows your zombie. Everyone is frozen during the 16s intro, then humans get 10s before the Alpha is released (`Classic.introLength`, `headStart` = 26).
